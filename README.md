# jung-gpt

JungGPT is a revolutionary emotional management app that helps you make sense of how you're feeling in that moment. JungGPT is trained on the entirety of the internets 
psychological, psychiatric, medical, and philosophical data and literature. JungGPT is not meant to be 
a replacement for therapy. The therapist client relationship, in our opinion, will always be more valuable than speaking to AI. In no way are the
developers insisting this be a replacement for, or a version of therapy.

This app is designed as a tool to help regulate your emotional state, it is not designed to form a connection, it does not have the intuition a 
human therapist or psychologist would.

All of this being said - not everyone has access to health care and mental health care. This app can be used as a tool to help discover more for yourself what you are feeling
and in identifying those emotions, help you act on feeling better or changing your situation.

Please use as intended
